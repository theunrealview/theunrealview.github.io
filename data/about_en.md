## Patio House | Single-Family Residence





##### A contemporary two-story residence designed to integrate indoor living spaces with the surrounding garden. Its architecture combines clean geometric volumes, natural stone cladding, light-colored surfaces, warm wood accents, and expansive glazing that enhances natural light and creates a seamless connection with the outdoors.





#### Architectural Concept

###### The design organizes everyday living around open social areas and clearly defined private spaces.

###### A carefully selected material palette introduces contrast and warmth, while terraces and covered outdoor areas extend the living spaces into the surrounding landscape.



#### Ground Floor

###### The ground floor features an entrance hall, staircase, open-plan living and dining area, kitchen with a central island, utility room, bathroom, and a flexible room that can serve as a bedroom or home office.

###### Large glass openings connect the main living spaces to the terraces, landscaped garden, and swimming pool.

###### The property also includes covered parking for two vehicles.



#### Upper Floor

###### The upper level is dedicated to privacy and relaxation, featuring a master bedroom with a walk-in closet and en-suite bathroom, additional bedrooms, a shared bathroom, and a central circulation area.

###### Balconies and outdoor extensions complement the interior spaces, reinforcing the connection between the residence and its surroundings.



#### Outdoor Spaces

###### The swimming pool, sun deck, outdoor lounge areas, and carefully designed landscaping create an inviting environment for relaxation and social gatherings.

###### Architectural lighting highlights the building's textures and geometric volumes, enhancing its atmosphere during the evening hours.
